Green room planner

Drag furniture on the floor plan, paint the walls from your swatches, and walk round the 3D view. Your changes are saved in this browser.

Tall furniture on the walls nearest the camera turns see-through (dashed) so it doesn't block the view. Whatever you've selected is outlined in orange.

Floor plan: drag things to move them

Click a piece to select it. R rotates it, the arrow keys nudge it 1 cm (Shift for 10 cm), and Delete takes it out of the room. Pieces snap to walls and to each other's edges. Click a wall to select it for painting, or click a 📷 to look from that corner.
